The 6 phases of the cycle

Each phase is delivered independently or as an integrated EPC package, with end-to-end documentation traceability and quality control.

Phase 01 · Exploration & survey

Locating and delineating the gas field

  • Geophysical campaigns and 2D/3D seismic acquisition.
  • Marine stratigraphy interpretation and geological modelling.
  • Reservoir characterisation and resource estimation.
  • Geotechnical and seabed site surveys for facility locations.
Phase 02 · Subsea drilling

Building the offshore well

  • Drilling campaign planning and well programme.
  • Subsea tooling, BOP and well control.
  • Coordination with rig contractors and directional services.
  • Logging, cementing and formation evaluation.
Phase 03 · Well completion

Getting the well ready to produce

  • Well completion and production string design.
  • Subsea wellhead and production tree (X-mas tree).
  • Perforating, stimulation and bringing the well onstream.
  • Safety barriers and well integrity testing.
Phase 04 · Subsea production

From the well to the process infrastructure

  • Subsea production systems (SPS): manifolds and jumpers.
  • Umbilicals, flowlines, risers and tie-backs to platform/FPSO.
  • Subsea control, flow assurance and hydrate inhibition.
  • Offshore installation and subsea hook-up.
Phase 05 · Processing & treatment

Conditioning the gas to specification

  • Phase separation (gas, condensate, water).
  • Dehydration and sweetening (H₂S and CO₂ removal).
  • Dew point adjustment and condensate/NGL recovery.
  • Compliance with commercial sales specification.
Phase 06 · Metering & export

Delivering the gas with traceability

  • Fiscal metering and production balance.
  • Compression and pumping for export.
  • Export via subsea pipeline or terminal.
  • Commercial documentation, custody transfer and compliance.
Cross-cutting disciplines

Engineering that supports the entire cycle

  • Offshore EPC: engineering, procurement and construction coordination.
  • QHSSE and asset integrity at every phase (well, subsea and process).
  • Project controls, vendor expediting and documentation control.
  • Inspection, QA/QC and technical audits.
  • Specialised manpower across upstream, midstream and offshore.
  • Commissioning, start-up and operational readiness.
