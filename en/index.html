Core specialty

Subsea gas exploration and production — full cycle

LINDSTROM KM's engineering core is to locate gas beneath the seabed, deploy extraction and operate the entire process: from geophysical exploration campaigns through to treated, metered gas ready for export. We integrate geoscience, subsea drilling, subsea production systems and processing into a single execution chain, with QHSSE and asset integrity at every phase.

01 Exploration & survey Geophysical and seismic studies, marine stratigraphy and reservoir characterisation to locate and delineate the field.
02 Subsea drilling Offshore drilling, subsea tooling, well control and coordination with subsea drilling contractors.
03 Well completion Well completion, subsea wellhead and production tree: the well ready to produce safely.
04 Subsea production Manifolds, umbilicals, flowlines and tie-backs to bring gas from the well to the process infrastructure.
05 Processing Separation, dehydration and sweetening (H₂S/CO₂ removal) up to commercial specification.
06 Metering & export Fiscal metering, compression and export by pipeline or terminal, with documentation traceability and compliance.
